Hold on to your money

Android Mac and Linux

Free and open source. No account, no cloud.

Made to help you save

HubidubiCash is here for one thing: to help you keep more of your money. It shows where your money went, whether you spent less than the week before, and which bills are still to pay. What you can see, you can change, and what you don't spend stays yours.

  • See where it goes

    The app opens on what went out this week, kind by kind, and whether it was more or less than the week before. A day, a month or a year, as you choose.

  • Bills, ticked off

    One month as a table: each income and bill on its day, ticked when it is paid. The life costs still to come run down day by day, and the total says what is left.

  • Regular costs add themselves

    Rent, a subscription, a bus pass: add it once. On its day each month it is in your spendings, without you lifting a finger.

A spending in three taps

The + is in the same place on every tab. It asks what you spent on, then how much.

  1. Press +

    Pick what kind of spending it was, type the amount, press Done. Once, or every month on the day you choose.

  2. Every spending, by day

    The last week, month or year, or the dates you pick. Fifty at a time, newest first, each day with its total.

  3. What is really left

    Income, bills as they come, and the life costs you still need this month. Tick what is paid. When everything is ticked, the next month shows.

Yours, and nobody else's

HubidubiCash has no internet permission at all. It cannot send your spending anywhere, because it cannot reach anywhere.

  • Never online

    No internet permission, on the phone or the computer. Everything stays on the device you typed it on.

  • Sealed backups

    Export makes one file, sealed with your password, to keep where you like or to move to another device. Without the password it is noise.

  • No account

    Nothing to sign up for, and no server of ours. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

On your computer too

The same app on a Mac or a Linux computer. Each device keeps its own spending; move it across with Export and Import.

Free on Android, Mac and Linux.
