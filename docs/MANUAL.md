# HubidubiCash manual

HubidubiCash is here to help you save money: to see where it goes, to keep
your bills in hand, and to know what is really left.

## Install

Android 8 or later, a Mac, or Linux on x86_64. Until the first release is in
the stores, build it from its source: see the README.

## Note a spending

Press **+** at the top right of Statistics, Spendings, Regular or Bills.
Pick what kind of spending it was: Food, Home, Transport, Utilities, Health,
Fun, Shopping or Other. Type the amount and press Done.

Under the amount you can add a note, and choose **Once** or **Every month**.
Once is spent today unless you pick another day; every month is spent on the
day of the month you choose, the 1st unless you pick another.

On Android:

- the **New spending** widget asks the kind, then the amount;
- a **kind widget** is for one kind, chosen when you place it, and goes
  straight to the amount;
- a long press on the app's icon offers **New spending** too.

## Statistics

Where the app opens: what went out today, in the last 7 days, the last month
or the last year (Settings › Statistics for), and whether that is more or
less than the same time before. Below it, each kind of spending as a bar, or
as a circle (Settings › Chart).

## Spendings

Every spending in the last week, month or year, or in the dates you pick,
newest first, under its day and the day's total. Fifty show at a time;
**Older** and **Newer** turn the page. Tap a spending to change or delete it,
or press and hold it.

## Regular

Your regular income and spending, all of them. A regular spending is added
to Spendings on its day each month, the first time on or after the day you
made it; if a day falls past the end of a short month, it is added on the
month's last day. If the app was not opened for a while, the months it missed
are added, each on its own day, and never twice.

Changing a regular item changes the months still to come. Deleting it stops
it; what it already added stays.

## Bills

One month as a table: each regular income and spending on its day, with a
box to tick when it has come in or is paid. A day in red has passed and is
not ticked yet. Under them, **Life costs**: what you need to live on for the
rest of the month (Settings › Life costs, 5 000 a month to begin with), less
each day. **Total** adds up every line.

When every income and bill of the month is ticked, the month is done and the
next one shows; **Undo** brings the last tick back for a few seconds.

## Settings

- **Statistics for**: today, the last 7 days, the last month or the last year.
- **Chart**: bars or a circle.
- **Life costs**: what you need a month to live on, for Bills.
- **Currency**: how amounts are shown. Changing it changes no amounts.
- **Language**: English, Polski, Deutsch, Français or Español.
- **Theme**: HubidubiTask's six. Ivory and Sand follow the system's light or
  dark mode.

## Backups

**Export** saves everything in the app to one file, sealed with a password
you type twice (at least 12 characters). Keep the file where you like, and
the password somewhere safe: without it the file can't be opened, by anyone.

**Import** reads such a file back, on this device or another, after showing
what it would add. What is newer here stays; nothing is added twice. Settings
are not in the file.

## Privacy

The app has no internet permission. Nothing you type leaves the device unless
you export it. See the privacy policy on the website.
