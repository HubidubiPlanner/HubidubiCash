# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — unreleased

- **Statistics first**: the last day, week, month or year by kind, as bars or
  a circle, compared with the time before.
- **A spending in three taps**: +, its kind, the amount. Once or every month.
- **Spendings** by day, 50 at a time, for a week, a month, a year or any dates.
- **Regular** income and spending; a regular spending adds itself on its day.
- **Bills**: the month as a table to tick off, with the life costs still to
  come and the total; the next month shows once all is ticked.
- **Widgets** on Android: New spending, and one for each kind you choose.
- **Sealed backups**: Export and Import, with a password.
- **Never online**: no internet permission, no account, no cloud.
- In English, Polski, Deutsch, Français and Español; HubidubiTask's six themes.
